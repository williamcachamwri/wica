If you build with AI coding assistants, you've probably spent hours comparing tools, LLM APIs and hosting options. I put together [Vibecoder Zone](https://vibecoder.zone/) to make that easier.


**What it is:** an online catalog for finding the right AI-coding stack. It lists 600+ tools across 36 categories, including:


- AI coding assistants
- LLM APIs
- Deployment, databases, auth and payments
- AI search, image and video generation
- Workflow automation and MCP servers


**What else is there:**


- Curated stacks, for example building a SaaS on a sub-$20 budget
- Leaderboards such as the Top Commercial LLMs Index and a Video AI Elo ranking
- Price comparisons, with prices checked regularly


The site itself was built with Cursor. If you're choosing tools for your next side project, give it a look and tell me what's missing: [https://vibecoder.zone/](https://vibecoder.zone/)
